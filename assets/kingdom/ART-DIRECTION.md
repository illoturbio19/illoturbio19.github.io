# The Quiet Conservatory

An original underground conservatory for Iu Tirbio's explorable portfolio. The visual reference is the hand-drawn gothic atmosphere of Hollow Knight, not its characters, maps, logos, music or other assets. Actual game artwork and video thumbnails elsewhere in the portfolio remain the respective projects' own media.

## Background

- Tool: built-in ImageGen, new image generation, opaque background.
- Runtime asset: `assets/kingdom/conservatory.webp`, 2172 x 724, 269866 bytes.
- The generated PNG was converted to WebP at quality 88 without changing its composition.

### Generation Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: actual playable 2D side-scrolling portfolio game background, original artwork inspired by the hand-drawn gothic subterranean atmosphere of Hollow Knight, NOT a screenshot or UI mockup. Generate an ultra-wide landscape 3:1 panoramic illustration, ideally 3072 by 1024. An abandoned moonlit underground conservatory with FOUR small distinct destinations spread along one continuous flat stone walkway at 82 percent image height. From left to right: a mossy carved arch with a stone portrait medallion at 15 percent; an abandoned inventor workshop with a ruby-red fabric awning and a glowing warm window at 38 percent; an elegant shrine holding pale turquoise crystal charms at 63 percent; a tall antique postal lantern and a tiny mailbox at 85 percent. Intricate hand-inked edges, layered distant vaulted aqueducts, thin hanging roots, readable architecture, deep charcoal ink rather than dominant blue, pale stone and silvery mist, muted moss green, tiny garnet red accents. Lower foreground black silhouettes of grasses and broken paving; middle distance structures clearly visible. Upper central 40 percent is atmospheric negative space with subtle distant arches, dark enough for white typography we will add in HTML. Side-on orthographic game camera, no perspective floor. Each destination should feel small relative to the vast architecture. Luminous but restrained, crisp environmental details, beautiful hand-painted 2D indie game art, elegant and quiet. No characters, no text, no letters, no logos, no interface elements, no circular bokeh orbs, no HUD, no copied characters or identifiable game assets. This asset will fill the entire interactive scene.

## Other Original Assets

- The lantern keeper is drawn in Phaser Graphics: a circular porcelain visor, green folded cloak, burgundy scarf and a small warm lantern. Four walking poses are generated locally; movement and jumping use Arcade Physics.
- The crest is a custom nested pointed arch and IT monogram in `crest.svg`. Its 180 px PNG variant is the Apple touch icon.
- Navigation remains HTML, including direct destination links, so the illustrated scene is never the only way to reach the work.
